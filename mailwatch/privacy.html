Privacy policy — MNAI mail watch

Last updated: 2026-11-09

What this app is

MNAI mail watch is a personal automation tool used by a single user (its owner) on the owner's own machines, in the owner's home.

Data accessed

The app accesses the owner's own Gmail messages using the read-only scope gmail.readonly. It cannot send, modify, or delete mail.

How data is used

Message metadata and content are processed locally on the owner's machines solely to decide whether a message is important enough to notify the owner about. Processing results (classification logs) are stored on the owner's machines.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party. No data is used for advertising. Data is not used to train machine-learning models.

Retention and security

OAuth credentials are stored on the owner's machine with owner-only file permissions and can be revoked by the owner at any time from the Google account's third-party access page. Classification logs are retained on the owner's machines under the owner's control.

Contact

The owner/operator can be reached at masato.nagai@gmail.com.
