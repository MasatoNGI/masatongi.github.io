MNAI mail watch

MNAI mail watch is a personal, single-user automation tool operated by its owner on the owner's own machines. It periodically reads the owner's own Gmail mailbox (read-only) and notifies the owner about important messages, such as execution notices and failure notices from financial institutions.

The tool is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not provide accounts or services to third parties.

Privacy policy

MNAI mail watch は、所有者本人が自分のマシン上で運用する、個人用の単一ユーザー自動化ツールです。所有者本人の Gmail メールボックスを読み取り専用で定期的に確認し、金融機関の約定通知や失敗通知など重要なメールを本人へ通知します。公開サービスではなく、第三者への提供は行いません。
