Privacy policy — MNAIDesk Gmail Push

Last updated November 20, 2026.

Who operates the app

The MNAI estate operator runs this private, single-user automation for the MNAIDesk Gmail mailbox.

Data accessed and used

The OAuth app uses the Gmail API’s gmail.metadata scope to read mailbox identity, message identifiers, history, headers and labels. This permission excludes message bodies and attachments and gives the OAuth app no ability to send, modify or delete mail.

Gmail sends change notifications through Google Cloud Pub/Sub. The notifications include the mailbox address and a Gmail history identifier. The watcher uses these notifications to find changes. The estate’s separate signed-in Gmail browser watcher can read message content when needed to classify an arrival while preserving its read state. Selected results are sent to the operator’s private message box.

Sharing and retention

Google provides Gmail and Pub/Sub for the notification path. The estate retains operational records and screened notices under the operator’s control. The operator does not sell Gmail data or use it for advertising or model training. The app’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access and deletion

OAuth credentials are stored on the operator’s machine with owner-only access. The operator can revoke the app’s access from Google Account connections and remove locally stored records. For privacy or deletion requests, contact mnaidesk@gmail.com.
